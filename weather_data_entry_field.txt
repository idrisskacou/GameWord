Weather entry data fields can vary depending on the specific application and requirements, but here is a comprehensive list of common fields:

1. **Date and Time**
   - Timestamp
   - Date
   - Time

2. **Location**
   - Latitude
   - Longitude
   - City
   - Country
   - Station ID

3. **Temperature**
   - Current Temperature
   - Minimum Temperature
   - Maximum Temperature
   - Feels Like Temperature

4. **Humidity**
   - Humidity Percentage

5. **Wind**
   - Wind Speed
   - Wind Direction
   - Wind Gusts

6. **Pressure**
   - Atmospheric Pressure

7. **Precipitation**
   - Precipitation Type (rain, snow, etc.)
   - Precipitation Amount

8. **Visibility**
   - Visibility Distance

9. **Cloud Cover**
   - Cloud Cover Percentage

10. **Weather Conditions**
    - Weather Description (clear, cloudy, rainy, etc.)
    - Weather Code (if using standardized weather codes)

11. **UV Index**
    - UV Index Value

12. **Sunrise and Sunset Times**
    - Sunrise Time
    - Sunset Time

13. **Dew Point**
    - Dew Point Temperature

14. **Air Quality**
    - AQI (Air Quality Index)
    - Pollutant Levels (PM2.5, PM10, CO, O3, NO2, SO2)

15. **Special Weather Alerts**
    - Alert Type (e.g., storm, heatwave)
    - Alert Description

These fields can be adjusted based on the specific needs of your weather tracking or reporting system.

Certainly! Here is a detailed list of data fields for the **Environmental Conditions** required for a space launch:

1. **Weather Conditions**
   - **Surface Temperature**
   - **Surface Wind Speed**
   - **Surface Wind Direction**
   - **Surface Humidity**
   - **Precipitation Type** (rain, snow, etc.)
   - **Precipitation Intensity**
   - **Visibility**

2. **Upper Atmosphere Conditions**
   - **Upper Wind Speeds** (at various altitudes)
   - **Upper Wind Directions** (at various altitudes)
   - **Temperature Profiles** (at various altitudes)
   - **Humidity Profiles** (at various altitudes)
   - **Pressure Profiles** (at various altitudes)
   - **Turbulence Levels**

3. **Solar Activity**
   - **Solar Radiation Levels**
   - **Geomagnetic Activity** (Kp index)
   - **Solar Flare Activity**
   - **Cosmic Ray Intensity**

4. **Lightning Activity**
   - **Lightning Frequency**
   - **Lightning Proximity** (distance from launch site)

5. **Cloud Cover**
   - **Cloud Base Altitude**
   - **Cloud Top Altitude**
   - **Cloud Thickness**
   - **Cloud Coverage Percentage**

6. **Marine Conditions** (if applicable for ocean launches or coastal launch sites)
   - **Sea Surface Temperature**
   - **Wave Height**
   - **Wave Direction**
   - **Current Speed**
   - **Current Direction**

7. **Air Quality**
   - **Concentration of Pollutants** (PM2.5, PM10, CO, O3, NO2, SO2)

8. **Thermal Conditions**
   - **Ground Temperature**
   - **Air Temperature at Different Altitudes**
   - **Dew Point**

9. **Launch Site Specific Conditions**
   - **Ground Moisture Level**
   - **Soil Temperature**
   - **Fog Presence and Density**

These data fields provide a comprehensive understanding of the environmental conditions that could impact the safety and success of a space launch.
